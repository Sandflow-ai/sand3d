## Build a first part {#start}

[Install sand3d and write a mounting bracket](start/install). Round its named top edge, drill two holes, and export it as a GLB.

note: The bracket from the first program.

## Find a verb {#why}

The [reference](reference/profiles) groups the API by family. Each entry gives its signature, parameters and limits. Examples show how to use the verbs together.

## Put parts together {#object}

[Concepts](start/concepts) explains names, frames and placement. [Writing an assembly](start/assembly) shows how to build a lamp and its shade, a rack of repeated parts, and a mirrored gate with hinges.

## Model a subject {#guides}

Follow a guide for [water](guides/water), [fire and smoke](guides/fire-smoke), [trees and vegetation](guides/trees), [terrain](guides/terrain), [organic forms](guides/organic), [colour](guides/colour), [glass and ice](guides/glass) or [mesh resolution](guides/resolution).

## Export a model {#export}

[Export and settings](reference/export) covers GLB, URDF, USD, IFC, OBJ and STL. Export scales program lengths to metres.

## Give the vocabulary to an LLM {#llm}

[For LLMs](llms) links to the API, assembly guidelines and task guides for a model's prompt.
